# HumanQA roadmap

Roadmap items are hypotheses tied to user evidence. Dates are intentionally omitted until maintainership capacity and validation exist.

## Now — prove v0.1 Proofrun

- Stable local declared-journey execution.
- Browser, network, console, trace, screenshot, DOM, axe, and keyboard evidence.
- Conservative deterministic findings and transparent release verdicts.
- JSON, Markdown, HTML, SARIF, JUnit, and safe generated-test scaffolds.
- Validated starter packs, portable skills, fixture twins, and CI examples.
- First-10-user concierge validation.

## Validate next

- Actionability and false-positive rate by rule.
- Time saved by generated scaffolds after review.
- Whether users adopt Quality Contracts or prefer ingestion of existing Playwright tests.
- Whether Keypath accessibility evidence drives more repeat use than the broader report.
- Pack adaptation time and contribution friction.
- Authentication-state import that preserves safety and privacy.

## Later, only with evidence

- Existing Playwright reporter ingestion.
- Signed community pack registry and compatibility matrix.
- Static evidence timeline.
- Encrypted team artifact sharing and retention controls.
- Framework route adapters and change-aware PR targeting.
- Reviewed ticket and CI integrations.
- Multi-contract release portfolio with accountable overrides.
- Optional provider adapters with field-level consent and local redaction review.
- Automatic issue creation as an opt-in, reviewed workflow.

## Explicit non-goals for v0.1

- Hosted SaaS dashboard.
- Autonomous exploration of arbitrary authenticated apps.
- Full visual-diff platform.
- Automatic GitHub issue creation or pull-request generation.
- Broad multi-agent orchestration.
- Dozens of unsupported persona and industry packs.
- Enterprise compliance certification.
- Claims of full accessibility compliance.
- Required expensive cloud analysis.
- Full test-management platform functionality.

The continue, narrow, pivot, and stop thresholds are in [docs/discovery/03-validation-plan.md](docs/discovery/03-validation-plan.md).
