# First 10 users

## Objective

Test whether a declared-journey evidence bundle improves one real authorized quality workflow. The goal is learning and artifact adoption, not testimonials.

## Cohort

- 2 startup engineers who own implementation and release.
- 2 QA engineers who can judge reproducibility and false positives.
- 2 developer-tool builders who can judge CLI and schema ergonomics.
- 2 product managers who can interpret criteria and release decisions.
- 1 accessibility practitioner who can challenge automation boundaries.
- 1 open-source maintainer who can test contribution and privacy surfaces.

Recruit 12–18 candidates to complete 10 sessions. Avoid a cohort composed entirely of friends, AI-tool enthusiasts, or one framework.

## Invitation

> I am testing an open-source local tool that turns one declared web journey into browser evidence, a release verdict, and a regression scaffold. I am looking for critical workflow feedback, not a testimonial. If you own or are authorized to test a local or staging application, would you bring one non-destructive journey to a 30-minute session? Please do not share production credentials or customer data.

## Session

1. Obtain note and artifact consent.
2. Ask the 10 neutral interview questions before showing the demo.
3. Let the participant choose the journey and describe current evidence.
4. Observe install and adaptation without taking control unless blocked.
5. Run HumanQA with safe mode, redaction, and local artifacts.
6. Ask the participant to explain verdict, evidence, limitations, and next action.
7. Offer test generation; observe whether they keep, edit, reject, or ignore it.
8. Ask what they would do tomorrow without suggesting integrations.

## Capture

Record role, environment type, setup time, first-audit success, journey setup time, definite/advisory finding counts, disputed findings, evidence opened, criterion comprehension, artifact adopted, privacy concern, next action, and exact pain wording with consent.

## Decision

Apply the CONTINUE, NARROW, PIVOT, and STOP thresholds in `docs/discovery/03-validation-plan.md` after 10 completed sessions. Publish anonymized aggregate results including failures and exclusions. Do not quote participants publicly without separate permission.
