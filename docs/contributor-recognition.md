# Contributor recognition plan

Recognition follows observable contribution, not status or follower count.

- Pack authors are preserved in YAML metadata and report attribution.
- Every release note credits merged code, documentation, fixture, research, accessibility, design, and community contributions with consent.
- First contributions receive a welcome note explaining where the work appears in the product.
- Recurring reviewers and mentors receive explicit stewardship credit in quarterly community notes.
- The `CONTRIBUTORS.md` list is opt-in and can use a preferred name and link.
- Significant design decisions credit the people who supplied evidence, including evidence that caused a feature to be removed or narrowed.
- Maintainer invitations consider sustained review quality, safety judgment, community care, and reliability rather than commit count alone.

Do not gamify raw issue, comment, or commit totals. Never fabricate contributor counts, adoption, testimonials, or impact.
